Project overview

Understand the EV market from multiple angles

This project brings together technical specifications, ownership economics, customer experience and market indicators to support exploration across vehicle types and regions.

Vehicle performance

Compare range, motor power, torque, acceleration, top speed and drivetrain configuration.

Battery & charging

Explore battery capacity, chemistry, cycle life, degradation and charging-time measures.

Cost & ownership

Review listed price, annual insurance cost, warranty and three-year resale-value estimates.

Market & impact

Investigate region, manufacturing country, annual sales, customer ratings and estimated CO₂ savings.

Dataset snapshot: The supplied CSV contains 100,000 records and 37 fields, including model year, manufacturer, market region, vehicle category, battery metrics, performance, pricing, safety, sales and customer rating. Summary figures describe the supplied dataset and are not independently verified real-world market statistics.

How to use this project

From raw data to actionable comparisons

The analysis is organized around the supplied dataset and the Tableau views published for this project.

01 / EXPLORE

Filter the vehicle landscape

Start with manufacturer, model year, category, manufacturing country or market region to narrow the comparison.

02 / COMPARE

Balance specifications and cost

Compare range, battery capacity, charging metrics, price, insurance, warranty and resale-value estimates.

03 / INTERPRET

Consider context and trade-offs

Use customer ratings, sales, safety ratings and estimated CO₂ savings as complementary indicators—not as a single universal ranking.

Note: The dataset includes modelled/synthetic-looking records and broad coverage across years and regions. Treat findings as dataset analysis unless the data source and collection methodology establish real-world representativeness.
